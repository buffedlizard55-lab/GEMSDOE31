## 3. Frozen `H31-M` Mathematical Specification & 3-Experiment Protocol

### 3.1 Label-Free Feature Channels (`src/gemsdoe31/h31m_features.py`)

All invalid/sentinel cells (`~isfinite` or `|x| >= 1e30`) are nearest-neighbor filled solely for spatial derivatives and strictly re-masked to `NaN` afterward. **Forbidden inputs:** `dist_cat_km`, `dist_sgmc_km`, `dist_vent_km`, `dist_probe_km`, `valid`, `labels.tif`, `derived_sgmc_faults_100m_u8.tif`, `derived_gdr_qfaults_v2_100m_u8.tif`.

- **`SUB_BASE_BANDS` (29 full-coverage bands):**
  `mag_anom`, `rtp`, `tmi`, `tmi_hg`, `tmi_vg`, `tc`, `TMI_up150`, `iso_grav_anom`, `iso_grav_anom_slope`, `iso_grav_anom_hg`, `iso_grav_anom_vg`, `depth_to_base_surf`, `cond_surf`, `geod_2ndinv`, `geod_shearrate`, `geod_dilaterate`, `deq_n100a15`, `ieq_n100a15`, `det_elev`, `det_elev_slope`, `K`, `Th`, `U`, `TC`, `ThK`, `UK`, `UTh`, `mean_k_500m`, `th_minus_k`.
- **`LIDAR_SCARP_BANDS` (11 continuous LiDAR scarp bands, `valid` excluded):**
  `ex_max`, `ex_mean`, `step_max`, `lapneg_max`, `lappos_max`, `downface_max`, `upface_max`, `cross_max`, `relief`, `coh100`, `upface_minus_downface`.
- **`H31M_CHANNELS` (8 new geophysical/structural channels):**
  1. `bstep_grad_norm`: $\|\nabla G_{1.5}(\text{depth\_to\_base\_surf})\| / (\max(\text{depth\_to\_base\_surf}, 0) + 100.0)$.
  2. `bstep_inflect`: $\max(0, -\lambda_{\min}(\nabla^2 G_{1.2}(\text{bstep\_grad\_norm})))$.
  3. `grav_tilt_zero`: $\exp(-|\theta_g| / 0.35) \cdot \|\nabla G_{1.0}(\theta_g)\|$, where $\theta_g = \arctan(\text{iso\_grav\_anom\_vg} / (\text{iso\_grav\_anom\_slope} + \epsilon_g))$ and $\epsilon_g = 0.01 \cdot \text{median}(\text{iso\_grav\_anom\_slope})$.
  4. `rtp_bandpass_crest`: $\|\nabla(G_{1.0}(\text{rtp}) - G_{4.0}(\text{rtp}))\|$.
  5. `cond_grad_ridge`: $\|\nabla G_{1.5}(\text{cond\_surf})\|$.
  6. `sub_tensor_coh`: $\sigma_{\text{int}} = 3.0\text{ px}$ structure-tensor anisotropy coherence $((\lambda_1 - \lambda_2)/(\lambda_1 + \lambda_2 + \epsilon))^2$ of the IQR-standardized sum $U_{\text{sub}}$ of (`bstep_grad_norm`, `grav_tilt_zero`, `rtp_bandpass_crest`, `cond_grad_ridge`).
  7. `surf_piedmont_ridge`: $\max(0, -\lambda_{\min}(\nabla^2 G_{2.0}(\text{det\_elev}))) / (1.0 + \max(\text{det\_elev\_slope}, 0) / 8.0)$.
  8. `xmod_strike_concordance`: $\sqrt{C_{\text{surf}} \cdot C_{\text{sub}}} \cdot \frac{1 + \cos(2(\phi_{\text{surf}} - \phi_{\text{sub}}))}{2}$ at $\sigma_{\text{int}} = 3.0\text{ px}$.

### 3.2 Deconfounded Dual-Head Learner & Bayes Prior-Odds Calibration

- Model class: `HistGradientBoostingClassifier(max_iter=140, learning_rate=0.08, max_leaf_nodes=31, min_samples_leaf=60, l2_regularization=2.0, early_stopping=False)`, positive weight `5.0`, negative weight `1.0`, negative cap `250,000`.
- Per fold `q` (`gemsdoe31-holdout-v1`, `COLLAR_M = 21_250.0 m`):
  1. Fit `M_sub` on `SUB_BANDS` over `train_allowed` (`catalogue & train_allowed` positives vs sampled `~catalogue & train_allowed` negatives). Apply exact training-only Bayes prior-odds normalization to reference prevalence $\pi_0 = 0.10$ using only training weighted counts.
  2. Fit `M_lidar` on `SUB_BANDS + LIDAR_SCARP_BANDS` strictly over `train_allowed & lidar_valid` (`step_max` finite), eliminating 3DEP tile-boundary confounding.
  3. On target cells $T$: compute `p_sub` everywhere; on `T & lidar_valid`, quantile-align `p_lid` onto `p_sub[T & lidar_valid]` and blend `p = 0.50 * p_sub + 0.50 * p_lid_aligned` (`p = p_sub` on `T & ~lidar_valid`).

### 3.3 Exact 29-Cell Lazy-Greedy Submodular `DTI` Kernel Allocator (`src/gemsdoe31/submodular_emit.py`)

Given target valid mask $V$, probability field $p$, fold-visible catalogue mask $C_{\text{vis}}$ (`catalogue & ~truth` in holdout; full `catalogue` in final OOF emission), and budget $K$:
- Smooth $p_s = G_{\sigma=0.8}(p)$; set excess weight $W(u) = \max(0, p_s(u) - \text{median}(p_s[V]))^{1.5}$ on $V$, with $W(u) = 0$ on $C_{\text{vis}}$ and smooth flank attenuation $\text{clip}((d(u, C_{\text{vis}}) - 1.0)/1.5, 0, 1)$ near $C_{\text{vis}}$.
- Restrict candidate dot centers $\mathcal{C} \subseteq \{c \in V : d(c, C_{\text{vis}}) > 2.0\text{ px}\}$ to the top `280,000` cells ranked by initial 29-offset kernel potential $\Delta F(c \mid \emptyset) = \sum_{m=1}^{29} k_m W(c + \delta_m)$ (`k_m` from `gemsdoe31.metric._offsets(100.0, 300.0)`).
- Maximize $F(S) = \sum_{u} W(u) \max_{i \in S} K(\|u - i\|)$ at $|S| = K$ via Minoux (1978) lazy-greedy priority queue with minimum center separation $d_{\min} = 2.25\text{ px}$ ($225\text{ m}$, permitting tight step-over/bend coverage inside the $300\text{ m}$ kernel while preventing adjacent-pixel redundancy).

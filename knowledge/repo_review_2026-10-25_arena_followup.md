## H33-2-B2: evidence-based answer, not score attribution

The local prior-art audit found that the H33-2-B2 support is D2.8 support with the dots within 2 pixels of the public catalogue removed. The comparison to H27-4 removes 2,545 dots while retaining the reported 35,483 dots more than 300 m from the catalogue. This is a geometric/pruning relationship, not evidence that H33 introduced a new geological detector. Exact lineage is recorded in `evidence/h33_forensics_20261025.json` and the nine-site audit.

A plausible explanation for a better reported score is that deleting predictions immediately adjacent to the public known-fault map reduces false-positive penalty while preserving much of the candidate support that can receive credit from hidden, off-catalogue faults. Under the prompt's stated alpha/beta weighting and spatial kernel, this mechanism is plausible; it does **not** establish the score cause. The owner's reported `0.2778` is not tied here to an organizer receipt and an exact-byte receipt was not found. The conflicting `0.3195` and `0.4413` values are likewise user-reported, not verified leaderboard observations. No claim that a new local holdout beats any of these values is justified: local HOLDOUT-DTI and reported competition scores are different evidence classes and are not numerically comparable.

**Can GEMSDOE31 beat 0.2778?** The evidence reviewed here cannot answer yes. No organizer-confirmed score, comparable same-protocol current-best reproduction, fresh confirmation, or eligible unique candidate is available. The probability of beating that score cannot be inferred from the sibling leaderboard list alone.

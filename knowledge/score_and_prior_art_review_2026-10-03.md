## What H27-4 appears to do—and what that does not prove

The GEMSDOE28 page describes H27-4 as a **one-pixel catalogue-flank prune** of the sparse D2.8 emission, not as a newly trained geological model. The page reports 44,090 positive cells for the D2.8 base and 40,199 for H27-4: a net removal of 3,891 cells, about 8.8% of the base set. It also describes the method as a blind prune, with no new per-pixel geology layer added.

A plausible mechanism is **precision/coverage trade-off under the official distance kernel**. Predictions near known mapped traces may be redundant, map-offset, or false positives; removing them can reduce false-positive mass while neighboring retained predictions preserve distance-weighted credit. The same operation can hurt if removed pixels cover genuine unmapped corrections near known faults. Because the private truth is not available here, those alternatives cannot be distinguished from the raster or the local proxy alone.

For a baseline DTI value `D`, the official Tversky form gives a useful marginal screen for adding predictions:

`ΔTPw / ΔFPw > αD / (1 − (1 − β)D) = 0.2D / (1 − 0.2D)`.

Conditionally, this threshold is about 0.0573 at the user-reported 0.2708 and 0.0683 at 0.3195. These are algebraic operating-point thresholds, **not** measured hit rates or forecasts. For deletions, the decision is the corresponding trade-off in lost weighted true-positive credit versus avoided false-positive mass. The model must be tested on independent, spatially separated evidence; score claims cannot stand in for that test.

The owner site also reports that H27-4's local paired holdout improvement replicated on a separate seed set. That is encouraging evidence about the *owner's catalogue-gap proxy*, but not proof that the prune improves generalization to faults absent from the catalogue. It is especially not evidence that the exact raster scored 0.2708.

## Reading the user-supplied score history conservatively

The supplied list suggests a useful research question: several reported improvements are associated with sparse emission, while broad or weakly selective outputs are reported lower. This is consistent with the official metric's nonzero false-positive penalty and distance-weighted credit, but cross-repository score comparisons are confounded by different rasters, labels, processing, submission identity, and unverified score attribution. They do not identify a causal feature or provide an unbiased holdout estimate.

The public 0.3195 figure and the 0.2708 H27-4 figure are not confirmed here. The additional historical score list supplied by the user is retained only as USER-REPORTED context; individual entries were not independently authenticated in this review, and no entry should be read as an official result. Even if any reported leaderboard value were correct, it is not a final private score or a measure of geologic truth. The official problem description states that the initial private round uses expert-labeled faults and that later final scoring uses an expanded expert-reviewed label set.

### Holdout decision: no top-candidate fit is authorized yet

The focal-mechanism candidate ranks first only as a **source-accessible desk hypothesis**. It was not trained or evaluated on a spatial holdout in this review. That is deliberate:

1. The prior H31-A pilot failed its paired catalogue-gap gate (mean ΔDTI −0.004009; 1/4 blocks positive), and the same-trace residual semivariograms failed the preregistered plateau criterion. The operational range and final buffer remain **unknown**. The unstable 24.964 km point fit and temporary 500 m pilot collar are not substitutes for a measured range.
2. This checkout has no `data/` competition inputs or `/tmp/gemsdoe25-full` sibling mirror/cache. The candidate cannot be fit reproducibly here. The DrivenData data tab is login-gated; no credentials or automated access were used.
3. The claimed H27-4 0.2708 result is not a verified incumbent, and the exact candidate raster is not in this checkout. A current-best comparator has not been reproduced on identical folds and a data-derived buffer.

Therefore **no candidate TIFF or weekly submission slot is approved**. Before any new model fit: reacquire authorized inputs and exact template provenance; make a new preregistration that preserves the H31-A stop; establish an identifiable out-of-fold residual range and its uncertainty; then run a paired, spatially blocked holdout against a reproduced incumbent and fresh confirmation. If the range is not identifiable, stop again rather than choose an arbitrary pixel buffer.

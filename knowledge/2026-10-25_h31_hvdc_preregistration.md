### Why `GEMSDOE32` Reached `0.2778` — And Why That Exact Lineage Cannot Reach `0.3195` or `0.4413`

1. **Multi-Line Physical Corroboration Base (`H19-5`):** Single-modality submissions across the broader 70-site history consistently collapsed (`LiDAR-only` `0.0383–0.0430`, `PF/basement-only` `0.0009–0.0984`, `SGMC-only` `0.0492–0.0512`, `relay-only` `0.0053–0.0159`, `continuous probabilities` `0.0041`) because lithologic contacts, erosional fan risers, and survey boundaries generate overwhelming false positives in any single physical channel. `H19-5` combined topographic scarp morphology, geothermal/alteration conduits, potential-field gradients, and fault-tip/step-over mechanics.
2. **Asymmetric Max-vs-Sum Structure of the Official DTI Metric:**
   $$\text{DTI} = \frac{\text{TP}_w}{\text{TP}_w + 0.2\,\text{FP}_w + 0.8\,\text{FN}_w} = \frac{\text{TP}_w}{0.8\,|G| + 0.2\,(\text{TP}_w + \text{FP}_w)}, \quad \text{TP}_w = \sum_{g \in G} \max_{x:\,d(x,g)\le 300\text{ m}} p(x)\,k(d(x,g))$$
   Because $\text{TP}_w$ applies a **pointwise maximum** over the 29 cells in each `300 m` disk whereas $\text{FP}_w$ **sums** every emitted positive cell linearly, dense contiguous fault lines (`121,131` px in `H19-5`) pay `~3×` redundant false-positive mass for overlapping `300 m` kernels. Sparse binary `{0, 1}` dots spaced near `250–300 m` retain ~76–85% of $\text{TP}_w$ at 31% of the pixel cost (`0.1922 -> 0.2600`).
3. **Zero-Credit Known-Catalogue Collar (`d_cat <= 200 m`):** Per official staff clarification (Forum Thread 11516 Post #4), public catalogue fault pixels are masked from evaluation. Because expert-mapped hidden traces are genuinely unmapped faults rather than `100–200 m` rasterization halos of already-compiled faults, dots inside `d_cat <= 200 m` earn below the fractional-programming inclusion threshold $\tau^* = \frac{\alpha \cdot \text{DTI}}{1 - \alpha \cdot \text{DTI}} \approx 0.0588$ weighted truth pixels/dot. Pruning `d_cat <= 2.0 px` (`-6,436` dots -> `37,654` dots) yielded `0.2778`.
4. **Why Post-Hoc Tweaks Hit a Hard Ceiling (and Violate Uniqueness):** Every one of the nine priority sites recycled `H19-5` (`100%` of `H33-2-B2` pixels are inside `H27-4` and `D2.8`). Further collar pruning (`B >= 3 px`) destroys real fault-tip splay and step-over coverage where `SGMC` off-catalogue credit exceeds break-even (`0.064 > 0.0588`), while bolting un-calibrated heuristic dots onto `H33` (`GEMSDOE36`, `+1,200` dots -> `0.2750`) dilutes precision. Reaching `>0.2778` toward `0.3195` / `0.4413` with a **genuinely unique** submission requires:
   - Training a **fresh out-of-fold multi-physics + hydrothermal-vent + structural accommodation probability surface** from scratch (eliminating `GEMSDOE31`'s earlier `lidar valid` survey-footprint confounder that starved the `24.63%` non-LiDAR footprint), and
   - Replacing heuristic BFS `dot_thin` with **exact 300 m triangular-kernel submodular maximum coverage** (`CELF` lazy greedy directly maximizing expected $\text{TP}_w$).

## 4. Frozen Experimental Protocol for `H31-HVDC` (`<= 3` Experiments, `< 2` Hours)

### 4.1 Spatially Buffered Hide-and-Recover Holdout (`gemsdoe31-hide-recover-v1`)
- **Spatial split:** 4 contiguous quadrants (`NW`, `NE`, `SW`, `SE`) split at the median row (`1851`) and column (`1639`) of the `5,167,373`-cell footprint.
- **Empirically derived spatial collar:** **`21,250 m` (`21.25 km`, `213` pixels)** around each held-out quadrant, taken directly from `evidence/buffer_derivation.json` (Matheron out-of-fold residual semivariogram along `3,199` 8-connected fault components). Every 8-connected fault component touching the test quadrant or its `21.25 km` collar is excluded from training.
- **Hide-and-recover draws:** In each `(quadrant, seed)` cell, whole 8-connected fault components are partitioned into `80%` `visible` and `20%` `hidden` (`hide_frac = 0.20`).
  - **Screen draws:** `seeds = [101, 102]` (`8` fold×draw cells).
  - **Fresh confirmation draws:** `seeds = [201, 202]` (`8` fold×draw cells, untouched by any feature/parameter choice).
- **Fold-visible-only rule:** Every catalogue-derived structural feature (`vis_tip_horsetail_lobe`, `vis_relay_bridge`, `vis_vent_tip_bridge`, and the `d_vis <= 2.0 px` exclusion collar) is recomputed **inside each cell strictly from `draw.visible`**, never from `draw.hidden_test` or `draw.hidden_train`. Any specialist using fold-visible features (`M_struct`) is trained strictly on `draw.hidden_train` whole withheld segments (`d_vis > 2.0 px`) vs buffered background negatives (`d_vis > 3.0 px`), never on `draw.visible`.
- **Scoring & Confidence Intervals:** Visible faults (`draw.visible`) are masked pixel-exactly (`pred[visible] = 0`). Pooled `HOLDOUT-DTI` (`alpha=0.2`, `beta=0.8`, `R=300 m` triangular kernel, 29 lattice offsets) is computed from pooled `(TPw, FPw, FNw)` across cells, with **95% confidence intervals** from `1,000` seeded `20 km × 20 km` spatial-tile cluster-bootstrap resamples (`seed = 20261025`).
- **Second Independent Truth Proxy (`OFFCAT-PROXY-DTI`):** Stitched 4-fold out-of-fold emissions (`37,654` binary `{0.0, 1.0}` dots, `d_cat > 2.0 px`) are also scored on the untouched `SGMC` off-catalogue state-map fault population (`61,664` pixels `> 300 m` from `labels.tif`). **Zero SGMC information is used anywhere in feature construction, training, calibration, or dot placement.**

### 4.2 Mandatory Canaries (Fail-Closed)
1. **Single-Feature Leakage Canary (Rule 4):** Every feature column in `H31-HVDC` (both static Group I and fold-visible Group II) is evaluated **alone** on the held-out `hidden_test` positives vs buffered background negatives across all 4 quadrants. Any feature with $\max(\text{AUC}, 1-\text{AUC}) > 0.90$ triggers an immediate leakage stop.
2. **Pre-Placement & Post-Placement Duplicate Canary (Rule 1):** Checked **both** on the stitched 4-fold continuous OOF surface before placement **and** on the final binary `{0.0, 1.0}` dot raster against all 4 `GEMSDOE31` registry rasters (`H27-4`, `D2.8`, `union-b-p060`, `add-arm-d28`), both committed `GEMSDOE31` OOF maps, and all 14 SHA-256-verified priority rasters across `19GEMSDOE`, `GEMSDOE24`, `25`, `27`, `28`, `30`, `32`, `33`, `36`, `70`. Stop if `|Spearman rho| > 0.90` OR `candidate dots within 3 px > 0.70` against any non-blanket registry raster.

### 4.3 Three-Experiment Budget (Rule 6)
- **`EXP-1` (Comparable Same-Run Control — `ArmD-Clean-Poisson`):** Clean 34-band baseline (`19` competition + `11` LiDAR without `valid` + `4` GeoDAWN rad + `4` GeoDAWN ext = 38 raw sensor channels, zero `valid` leakage), 4-fold `21.25 km`-buffered `HistGradientBoostingClassifier`, emitted via Hessian ridge priority `2.8 px` Poisson-disk thinning at exact `37,654` global budget (`~0.7287%` of footprint per quadrant, `d_vis > 2.0 px`).

## Preregistered scientific mechanism

Four hypotheses were ranked before fitting in `knowledge/2026-10-25_magrest_preregistration.md`: (1) reciprocal magnetic texture restoration (MAGREST), (2) GRAVREST magnetic/gravity co-restoration, (3) RADREST multichannel ranked radiometric texture restoration, and (4) TERRREST detrended drainage-pattern restoration. Only MAGREST was implemented; the others remain untested, qualitatively ranked hypotheses, not score forecasts. No new external data were needed; an idea requiring unavailable fresh official data was not asserted viable.

RTP magnetics B2 are band-passed with Gaussian sigma .75 minus 4 px. Across four tangent orientations, opposite flanks are sampled ±3 normal pixels and matched with local normalized cross-correlation (sigma 1.25). Signed ±2 and ±4 tangent restoration improvements are multiplied by positive match and absolute Gaussian-2 local mean of preferred shift sign. A 20 px finite-data/edge guard suppresses missing-data seams. The primary is the frozen same-orientation/same-sign geometric mean of the two scales. Diagonal sampling distances are sqrt(2) longer; this is disclosed, not a circularly invariant physical-slip estimator.

The signature may reveal offset geophysical texture without needing an already mapped fault. It is a hypothesis, not a discovery map. **Named mimics:** volcanic flow/contact juxtaposition, undisturbed anisotropic magnetic fabric, lithologic boundaries and flight-line seam/corrugation. Synthetic tests establish behavior of a correct-orientation correlation primitive, not geological specificity of the max-over-orientation detector. A pre-score invalid specificity assertion was fixed before feature/scientific evaluation; no post-holdout feature tuning occurred.

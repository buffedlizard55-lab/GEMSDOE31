## Fixed MAGREST operator and three experiments

Clean B2 with the shared cache. For filtering, nearest-fill missing values; restore a validity mask with a **20-pixel edge/missing-data guard** to avoid matching synthetic boundary fills. Use a high-pass image `z = Gaussian(rtp, 0.75) - Gaussian(rtp, 4)`.

For oriented unit-grid tangent vectors `(0,1), (1,0), (1,1), (1,-1)`, let `n=(-t_col,t_row)`. Compare Gaussian-localized patches (sigma 1.25 pixels) centered at `x-3n` and `x+3n+l*t`. Normalize their covariance by the product of their local standard deviations, with scale-safe epsilon. The diagonal physical distances differ by sqrt(2); record that this is a grid-direction bank, not isotropic physical-length sampling.

For each offset magnitude `d`, compare `l in {-d,0,+d}`. `gain=max(rho_-d,rho_+d)-rho_0`; require positive matched correlation and positive gain. Multiply by absolute local mean (sigma 2) of the preferred signed-shift map to penalize inconsistent restorations. Max over four orientations.

- **Experiment 2 MAGREST-4:** offset magnitude 4 pixels (diagonal 566 m).
- **Experiment 3 MAGREST-concordant:** within the SAME orientation require the two offset banks to prefer the same sign; geometric mean of the two responses; max over orientations. **Primary packaging arm fixed in advance = experiment 3**, not a post-hoc best-arm selection.

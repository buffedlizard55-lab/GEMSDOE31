## 3. Selected Lane (`H31-E · SCGP-Submod`) — Frozen Experimental Design

### 3.1 Named Non-Fault Mimic & Physical Rejection Mechanism
- **Named non-fault mimic**: **Pleistocene Lake Lahontan wave-cut paleoshoreline terraces** (~1,200–1,335 m elevation contours) and **unfaulted volcanic flow margins / erosional fan channels**, which create sharp 1 m LiDAR steps or shallow magnetic edges without deep basement offset.
- **Physical rejection mechanism**: A wave-cut shoreline follows an equipotential elevation contour ($\nabla \text{elev}$ perpendicular to shoreline, zero basement-depth step, discordant with $\nabla g_{\text{iso}}$), whereas a tectonic fault cuts across elevation contours along a linear structural strike corroborated by parallel gravity–basement cross-gradients ($C_{\text{grav,base}}$), hydrothermal K/Th or thermal anomalies, and en-echelon structural continuity. We include an explicit contour-bench detector (high local `step_max` with near-zero along-strike elevation change and discordant potential-field strike) so the gradient-boosted learners can down-weight paleoshoreline mimics.

### 3.2 Empirically-Buffered Whole-Segment Spatial Holdout (`scripts/evaluate_holdout.py`, evaluator `gemsdoe31-hide-v2.0`)
1. **Spatial blocks & empirical buffer**: Partition the `3730 × 3292` grid into four contiguous quadrants (`NW`, `NE`, `SW`, `SE` about the footprint centroid). Each test quadrant is eroded by `12 px` (`1,200 m`) from interior split lines so no `300 m` metric kernel straddles a fold boundary. Training for test quadrant $q$ excludes **all pixels in quadrant $q$ PLUS the empirical `21,300 m` (`213 px`) residual-semivariogram collar** derived in `evidence/buffer_derivation.json` (`21.25 km` rounded up to integer 100 m cells), AND excludes any 8-connected fault component touching quadrant $q$ or its 21.3 km collar.
2. **Whole-segment hide-and-recover inside each test quadrant**: Connected components (`8`-connectivity) of `labels == 1` inside test quadrant $q$ are partitioned by deterministic seeded sampling so `30%` of catalogue fault pixels (composed strictly of **whole 8-connected fault segments**) form `hidden_test`, and the remaining `70%` of whole segments form `visible_faults`.
   - **Screen stage (`Experiment 1` & `Experiment 2`)**: Frozen seed `draw = 101`.
   - **Fresh confirmation stage (`Experiment 3`)**: Untouched fresh seeds `draws = [202, 303]`, executed with zero hyperparameter or feature modifications.
3. **Strict visible-only catalogue features & exact masking**:
   - Static physical features use **zero** fault labels and **zero** SGMC data (`dist_cat_km` and `dist_sgmc_km` are explicitly banned and verified absent).
   - Fold-dynamic structural features (`vis_tip_lobe`, `vis_relay_bridge`, `vis_annular_density_5km`, and `d_vis > 200 m` deadweight flank suppression) are computed **exclusively from `train_visible_faults` on training folds** and **exclusively from `test_visible_faults` (`catalogue & ~hidden_test`) on test quadrants**.
   - During test evaluation, `visible_faults` pixels are masked pixel-exactly (`pred[visible_faults] = 0`, excluded from truth) per the official DrivenData staff clarification, and **pooled DTI** ($\alpha = 0.2, \beta = 0.8, R = 300\text{ m}$ over the 29 exact kernel offsets) is computed across all 4 folds with a **1,000-replicate component-stratified bootstrap 95% CI**.
4. **Leakage Canary (`Rule 4`)**: Before fitting any model on `draw = 101`, every individual static and fold-visible feature is scored **alone** (`ROC-AUC` against `hidden_test` vs background across the 4 test quadrants). Any feature with `AUC > 0.90` halts execution as suspected leakage.

### 3.3 Three-Experiment Budget (`Rule 6`, `<= 3` Experiments)
- **Baseline Comparator (`Arm 0 · Baseline-D-Poisson`)**: Same-fold, same-sample `HistGradientBoostingClassifier` (`max_iter=200, learning_rate=0.07, max_leaf_nodes=31, min_samples_leaf=60, l2_regularization=2.0`) on the existing 39 raw multi-layer bands (`19 competition + 12 LiDAR + 8 GeoDAWN`), emitted with `d_vis > 200 m` flank mask and score-ordered `2.8 px` Poisson-disk thinning at the fixed global density (`37,654 / 5,167,373 = 0.7287%` of scored footprint, matching the empirically optimal `37,654` full-grid dot budget).
- **Experiment 1 (`H31-E · SCGP-Submod`, Screen `draw = 101`)**: Seamless Dual-Expert `HistGradientBoostingClassifier` (Full-Footprint Multi-Physics Expert + Rank-Matched 1 m LiDAR Polarity Expert) on the `H31-E` multi-physics + fold-visible structural feature stack, emitted via **1D Ridge-Localized Lazy-Greedy 300 m Submodular Expected-DTI Allocation** (`min_dist = 2.2 px`, `d_vis > 2.0 px`, same exact dot budget).
- **Experiment 2 (`H31-E-Poisson` Ablation, Screen `draw = 101`)**: Exact same `H31-E` Dual-Expert probability field emitted with standard `2.8 px` Poisson thinning (`Baseline` emitter), isolating (a) the multi-physics + dual-expert surface gain and (b) the 300 m submodular kernel allocator gain.
- **Experiment 3 (`H31-E · SCGP-Submod` Fresh Confirmation, `draws = [202, 303]`)**: Frozen `H31-E · SCGP-Submod` vs `Baseline-D-Poisson` on two completely fresh, unseen whole-segment holdout draws (`202` and `303`), plus full-footprint 21.3-km-buffered OOF emission (`37,654` off-catalogue dots), residual semivariogram re-check, dual off-catalogue proxy check (`SGMC \ cat` and `Piedmont SGMC \ cat`), duplicate canary check (both continuous surface and final dots vs all 16 local + peer registry rasters), and GeoTIFF packaging via `scripts/submission_writer.py`.

### 3.4 Automated Promotion & Submission-Readiness Gates (Zero Human Inspection Required)
A generated GeoTIFF receives `verdict = "promote"` and `automated_validation_passed = true` **if and only if ALL 5 machine-checked gates pass**:
1. **G1 — Format & `[0, 1]` Range Gate**: Single-band `float32`, `EPSG:32611`, `3730 × 3292`, exact Affine transform, `0` NaN/Inf inside the `5,167,373` footprint cells, all footprint values in `[0.0, 1.0]`, exact `NaN` outside footprint (`-nan.tif`) and exact `0.0` outside footprint (`-allfinite.tif`).
2. **G2 — Single-Feature Leakage Canary Gate**: Every feature alone has holdout `ROC-AUC <= 0.90`.
3. **G3 — Pre-Placement Surface & Final-Dot Uniqueness Gate**: Against **all 4 local `GEMSDOE31` registry rasters and all 12 pinned peer rasters (`GEMSDOE24..36, 70`)**, Spearman rank correlation `<= 0.90` AND directed 3-px dot overlap `<= 70%` on **both** the continuous surface before placement and the final `37,654` dots.
4. **G4 — Empirically-Buffered Hide-and-Recover Holdout Screen & Fresh Confirmation Gate**: `H31-E · SCGP-Submod` beats `Baseline-D-Poisson` by `> +0.002` pooled `HOLDOUT-DTI` on Screen (`draw = 101`), wins `>= 3/4` spatial quadrants, AND beats `Baseline-D-Poisson` with a strictly positive 95% bootstrap CI on Fresh Confirmation (`draws = [202, 303]`).
5. **G5 — Dual Off-Catalogue Discovery Gate**: Full-footprint OOF emission beats both `Baseline-D-Poisson` and the `H33-2-B2` (`0.0940`) reference on **both** off-catalogue `SGMC \ {d_cat <= 300 m}` AND piedmont-regime off-catalogue `SGMC` (`det_elev <= 50 m`).

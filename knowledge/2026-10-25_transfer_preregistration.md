1. On cells with both bands finite inside the template footprint, robustly standardize each field by its footprint median and IQR (floor 1e-6). These are label-free normalizations, not physical unit inversions.
2. Fill missing cells only for filtering with the finite median (zero in standardized units); remove the Gaussian regional trend at sigma=20 pixels. A full valid halo of `ceil(4*20 + 4*7 + 2) = 110` pixels is required for nonzero output; unsupported cells predict zero, never NaN. Record the coverage lost to this deliberately conservative rule.
3. For sigma in {3,7} pixels, compute local means, covariance C, and nonnegative variances VM/VG by Gaussian convolution. Let lambdaM = 0.05*median(VM>0), lambdaG analogously (floor 1e-8), determined from valid, unlabeled cells only.
4. Define b=C/(VM+lambdaM), theta=atan(b), J=|gradient(theta)|, and normalize J by its valid 95th percentile, clipping this *feature* to [0,1]. Define Q=clip(C^2/((VM+lambdaM)*(VG+lambdaG)),0,1). This is a regularized coupling-strength descriptor, not a calibrated R-squared statistic.

Experiment 05

Addition arm: incumbent plus off-catalogue detections

Does adding high-probability off-catalogue detections to the incumbent improve the off-catalogue proxy?

Measured, weak positiveWeakly positive. The best union adds 15,105 dots for +0.0017 on the proxy — the right sign, but within the owner's observed ±0.003 live noise, so it is a research candidate and not slot-approved.

The incumbent was unioned with cells where the fold-D detector probability exceeded a threshold and the cell was not on the public catalogue, at thresholds from 0.5 to 0.95. Adding pixels is directly invertible, unlike subtracting them: the surface is flat away from the incumbent, so each added dot's contribution is measurable.

## Evidence-qualified explanation of H33-2-B2's reported 0.2778

- **USER-REPORTED, not ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED:** 0.2778 is the owner-supplied claim. No exact organizer score receipt was found. The conflicting 0.3195 and 0.4413 anchors also remain unverified. No leaderboard was accessed.
- **COMPUTED raster geometry:** `evidence/h33_forensics_20261025.json` records 44,090 D2.8 pixels, 40,199 H27 pixels, and 37,654 H33 pixels. H27 is D2.8 with dots within one catalogue pixel removed; H33 is H27 with a further 2,545 dots removed inside two catalogue pixels. H33 adds no pixels to H27. H33 has zero pixels on catalogue traces and its nearest catalogue distance is 2.236 pixels (about 282.8 m at 100 m pixels).
- **Plausible metric mechanism — inference, not causal proof:** the 300 m triangular-kernel DTI rewards covered fault truth while charging redundant prediction mass. Removing catalogue-flank dots and retaining a kernel-scale sparse pattern could reduce false-positive cost more than it loses true-positive credit. The private truth is unavailable, so the files cannot establish whether the deleted dots were false positives or why the reported score changed. H33 is therefore a catalogue-distance prune, not evidence of a new geological detector.
- The metric identity in the forensic record gives the pruning condition in terms of lost truth credit and removed false-positive cost. It does not turn 0.2778 into training truth, a holdout result, or a forecast. No new-candidate estimate can be inferred from this geometry.

H31-A
Multiscale magnetic local-wavelength discontinuity		 Supplied tmi_hg and tmi_vg. Build analytic-signal amplitude and a scale-normalized local characteristic-length proxy at σ=1 and 3 cells, then a cross-scale edge response. Basement/contact offsets can change magnetic response wavelength and edge geometry without a mapped surface scarp. Cross-scale persistence may suppress single-scale noise. Not another amplitude, tilt, or single-ridge feature. Tests local characteristic wavelength and its transition across two fixed scales; no catalogue geometry enters static feature creation. +0.000 to +0.006, low confidence; ~1 day. Existing hash-pinned bands only. Mirror provenance, not organizer-authenticated input.

H31-B
Raw LiDAR return attributes		 USGS 3DEP/GeoDAWN LAZ return density, intensity quantiles and cross-scarp contrasts aggregated to the 100 m grid. Near-surface material, roughness or cover changes can mark a subtle damage zone when a bare-earth scarp edge is weak; vegetation, roads, mines and acquisition geometry are confounders. Prior work uses derived DEM/scarp descriptors; it does not test raw per-return point-cloud attributes. +0.001 to +0.015, very uncertain; 3–5 days after data access. TNM API lists one in-footprint tile record, but the LAZ binary/header/attributes and full-grid overlap remain unverified.

Depth-resolved MT conductance continuity		 Five published magnetotelluric conductance depth ranges across 2–200 km; test cross-depth persistence of gradients/lineaments. A fault or fluid pathway may connect deep conductive anomalies without a mapped surface trace; MT resolution and non-uniqueness may overwhelm a 100 m target. Tests depth-stack coherence, not just supplied surface conductivity or conductive-base depth. 0 to +0.008, low confidence; 2–4 days after retrieval. Public GDR source checked; individual rasters, resolution and exact GEMS coverage not verified.

Frozen experiment · pilot outcome

H31-A: design and paired pilot result

Two feature columns, no labels in feature creation

Use the pinned owner-mirror values of tmi_hg and tmi_vg. The local characteristic length is a wavelength/scale proxy—not a physical depth estimate. Smooth at σ=1 and 3 grid cells, calculate the length response, then create:

  • H31_mag_local_length
  • H31_mag_scale_edge

Invalid derivative cells are nearest-filled only during filtering, then re-masked. Normalization uses unlabeled values in the footprint.

Same-run paired comparison

Base features: BDE plus fixed X1–X3 add-ons (same-run H19-5 parent comparator). Candidate: same columns plus the two H31-A features. Same folds/draws, negative samples, HistGradientBoosting model, emission and DTI.

Use four contiguous quadrants, whole connected known-fault components hidden at a fixed 20% proxy rate, and the exact official 300 m distance-weighted Tversky endpoint. This is a catalogue-gap proxy, not private truth.

Why the final buffer remains unknown

Pilot completed

Draw 10 · temporary 500 m collar

The 500 m collar generated held-out residuals only. It is not an empirical range and must never be reused as the final buffer.

Fit attempted

Same-trace residual variogram

Each arm had 583 trace groups with pairs and 28 populated 500 m bins, but observed support ended at 13.75 km. The fit reached only 0.808 of the fitted partial sill; the stability rule requires 0.95.

Stop · range unknown

No final buffer

The unstable ~24.964 km point-fit diagnostic is unsupported extrapolation; no bootstrap interval was produced. Operational range and final buffer are unknown. H31-A stops.

Promotion gate

A screen pass is not enough

  • Screen draws 11–12: paired mean DTI gain strictly above +0.001; candidate better in at least 3/4 blocks; worst block no worse than −0.010; mean catalogue-hug increase no more than +0.10; stable final buffer.
  • Re-fit variograms on final-screen residuals. If the upper range exceeds the applied buffer, enlarge and rerun the registered screen, up to the preregistered limit.
  • Fresh confirmation draws 13–14 use the same exact frozen buffer and must pass the same metric/buffer checks. Confirmation failure stops the candidate.
  • H31-A is stopped before any weekly-slot gate. A future candidate would still need to reproduce and beat the actual current-best comparator on the same folds/buffer, pass fresh confirmation, and pass exact-file validation; none of those promotion gates is satisfied.

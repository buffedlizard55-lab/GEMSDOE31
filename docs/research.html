H33: a precise geometric explanation, not private-truth causation

Byte-pinned comparisons establish H33 = H27 with predictions at catalogue distance ≤ 2 px removed. H27 itself equals D2.8 with predictions at distance ≤ 1 px removed. H33 adds zero predictions and deletes 2,545 from H27, leaving 37,654. The closest retained catalogue distance is √5 px; minimum dot-to-dot spacing is about 283 m, median 300 m.

Mechanistic inference: thinning limits redundant distance-kernel coverage; deleting catalogue-flank predictions can remove false-positive penalty while preserving credit to off-catalogue truth. But it can also delete useful private-truth detections. The condition is quantitative, not a promise:

S = T / (0.2 T + 0.2 F + 0.8 K + epsilon)
A deletion improves S iff:
lost_T < [0.2 S / (1 - 0.2 S)] × removed_F

T is truth-weighted maximum credit, F is prediction mass minus nearest-truth self-credit, and K is truth count. Private truth is unavailable, so the inequality cannot be established for the live competition from these rasters.

GEMSDOE32's symmetric/calibrated LM instrument is not this pooled max-credit evaluator; its numbers are not comparable. There is no evidence-based forecast that this new file beats any of those reported values.

TGJ31 mechanism and negative outcome

Median/IQR-normalized RTP and gravity are regionally detrended. A regularized local gravity-on-magnetics coefficient is transformed with arctan; its gradient must persist at both 3 and 7 px scales, weighted by coupling strength. A 110 px valid-data halo avoids sentinel and missing-data edges. There is no catalogue or SGMC predictor, no supervised fitting and no inherited prediction input. This is a bounded physical response, not a calibrated probability or a source inversion.

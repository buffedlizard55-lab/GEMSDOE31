Multiscale magnetic local-wavelength discontinuity		 Supplied tmi_hg and tmi_vg. Build analytic-signal amplitude and a scale-normalized local characteristic-length proxy at σ=1 and 3 cells, then a cross-scale edge response. Raw LiDAR return attributes		 USGS 3DEP/GeoDAWN LAZ return density, intensity quantiles and cross-scarp contrasts aggregated to the 100 m grid. Near-surface material, roughness or cover changes can mark a subtle damage zone when a bare-earth scarp edge is weak; vegetation, roads, mines and acquisition geometry are confounders. Tests depth-stack coherence, not just supplied surface conductivity or conductive-base depth. 0 to +0.008, low confidence; 2–4 days after retrieval. Frozen experiment

H31-A: what is being tested

Two feature columns, no labels in feature creation

Use the pinned owner-mirror values of tmi_hg and tmi_vg. The local characteristic length is a wavelength/scale proxy—not a physical depth estimate. Smooth at σ=1 and 3 grid cells, calculate the length response, then create:

  • H31_mag_local_length
  • H31_mag_scale_edge

Invalid derivative cells are nearest-filled only during filtering, then re-masked. Normalization uses unlabeled values in the footprint.

Same-run paired comparison

Base features: BDE plus fixed X1–X3 add-ons (same-run H19-5 parent comparator). Candidate: same columns plus the two H31-A features. Same folds/draws, negative samples, HistGradientBoosting model, emission and DTI.

Use four contiguous quadrants, whole connected known-fault components hidden at a fixed 20% proxy rate, and the exact official 300 m distance-weighted Tversky endpoint. This is a catalogue-gap proxy, not private truth.

No arbitrary pixel collar

How the final buffer will be measured

Pilot only

Draw 10 · temporary 500 m collar

The pilot collar obtains held-out residuals only. It is not described as an empirical range and is never the final setting by default.

Fit dependence

Same-trace residual variogram

Compute Matheron semivariance of y − p only on hidden known-fault pixels; pair within the same 8-connected component, fold and draw. 500 m bins to 50 km; at least 20 pairs per bin and 30 trace groups; 200 component-bootstrap replicates.

Use uncertainty

Largest upper range wins

Final buffer is the 100 m-rounded-up maximum upper 95% practical-range bound across base and H31-A, with a 300 m metric-support floor. If a stable sill/range cannot be identified, buffer is unknown and the experiment stops.

Promotion gate

A screen pass is not enough

  • Screen draws 11–12: paired mean DTI gain strictly above +0.001; candidate better in at least 3/4 blocks; worst block no worse than −0.010; mean catalogue-hug increase no more than +0.10; stable final buffer.
  • Re-fit variograms on final-screen residuals. If the upper range exceeds the applied buffer, enlarge and rerun the registered screen, up to the preregistered limit.
  • Fresh confirmation draws 13–14 use the same exact frozen buffer and must pass the same metric/buffer checks. Confirmation failure stops the candidate.
  • Before any weekly slot: reproduce the current comparable incumbent on the same folds/buffer, beat it, pass fresh confirmation, and pass exact-file validation. No such result exists yet.

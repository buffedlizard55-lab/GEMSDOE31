These are portal instructions, not permission to submit the current research file.

  1. Confirm the generated readiness receipt says ok_to_submit: true for the exact file. Today it says false.
  2. Download the approved .tif or the ZIP containing that single TIFF. Do not upload a report, run-card JSON, input feature stack or multi-file ZIP.

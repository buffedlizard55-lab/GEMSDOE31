COMPUTED · local mirror geometry

D2.8 is sparse emission, not a new trained model

The local raster contains 44,090 positive cells and is pixel-identical (not byte-identical) to the pinned sibling D2.8 artifact. That owner record describes deterministic thinning of H19-5. The parent has 121,131 positives; the owner-reported D1.5 variant has 60,069.

Under the official 300 m triangular kernel, thinning can remove redundant predictions and reduce false-positive mass, but it can also remove unique fault coverage. The tradeoff depends on hidden truth, which the raster cannot reveal.

Conditional model, not a score

One first-order owner-mirror model predicts about 0.2553 for D2.8 with a 0.2500–0.2613 sensitivity band under assumed geometry and two unverified score anchors. The band is not a statistical confidence interval. A separate owner package summary gives a conditional estimate around 0.251. The difference and the failed H28 proxy gate reinforce that these are assumption-sensitive model outputs, not official competition scores.

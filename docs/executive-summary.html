How to enter a submission

These are portal instructions, not permission to submit the current research file.

  1. Confirm the generated readiness receipt says ok_to_submit: true for the exact file. Today it says false.
  2. Download the approved .tif or the ZIP containing that single TIFF. Do not upload a report, run-card JSON, input feature stack or multi-file ZIP.
  3. Use your own DrivenData competition page to open New submission, accept the rules and check the remaining weekly quota. This project does not know or spend your current quota.
  4. Under File to submit, select that approved file. Use its generated unique name and paste its note (at most 140 characters) into Note (optional).
  5. Only after approval and quota checks, submit. Keep the organizer's receipt, exact SHA-256, name and reported result together. Until that receipt exists, no result is ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED.

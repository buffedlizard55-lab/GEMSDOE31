Validation methods

Why the buffer is measured

Spatially blocked validation is motivated by peer-reviewed guidance on structured dependence. These methods papers do not prescribe a universal pixel distance for GEMS; this project estimates the residual range empirically on same-trace held-out fault pixels.

  • Roberts et al. (2017), Cross-validation strategies for data with temporal, spatial, hierarchical, or phylogenetic structure.
  • Valavi et al. (2019), blockCV: spatially or environmentally separated folds.

A fixed 4–5-pixel collar is not assumed. If the empirical variogram is unstable, the range and final buffer remain unknown and the experiment fails closed.

Evidence classification

What each label means

OFFICIAL

Contest administrator, DOE/NLR, USGS, NASA or primary release statements.

OWNER-MIRROR

Bytes/claims from a pinned owner repository. Hashes establish mirror identity only.

COMPUTED

Local test, hash, raster or experiment output with code/data provenance.

INFERENCE

A geological mechanism or model expectation to test, not a private-label finding.

Operating principles

Maximize P(Win) · Own the Outcome

Spend effort where it can change a decision—and publish negative or blocked results when that is what the evidence supports.

Maximize P(Win)

Do not burn a weekly slot on an unconfirmed proxy win. Compare against the reproduced current-best holdout comparator on the same buffered folds, then reserve fresh draws for confirmation.

Own the Outcome

Keep hashes, test receipts, source limitations, and score claims auditable. Unknown remains unknown; a model expectation is never presented as an official result.
